## 🗺️ Stage 0 — Concept Map

- **The Problem:** Rapid AI adoption has created a gap between what AI systems can do and what feels intuitive to users. Many treat AI as a trivial "spellchecker" or rely on fragile prompt hacks that break across model updates.
- **Where It Fits:** Introductory baseline for the entire Claude Certified Architect curriculum.
- **Exam Relevance:** Understand the philosophy of human-AI collaboration and why lasting frameworks outlive prompt tricks.

---

## 🔑 Key Terms

| Term | Exam Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AI Fluency** | The discipline of interacting with AI systems effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely. |
| **Thinking with AI** | Shifting from treating AI as a passive autocomplete tool to treating it as a collaborative problem-solving partner. |
| **The Intuition Gap** | The divide between raw model capability and human intuition on how to direct it properly. |
| **The 4D Framework** | Anthropic's core competencies: **Delegation**, **Description**, **Discernment**, and **Diligence**. |

## 🎈 Core Philosophy: From Tool to Partner

```text
OLD MENTAL MODEL:  User ──► [ "Fix my grammar / spellcheck" ] ──► Passive Tool
NEW MENTAL MODEL:  User ◄──► [ Collaborative Co-Reasoning ]   ──► AI Partner
```

- **Human-Centric Collaboration:** Focuses on how humans direct and collaborate with AI systems rather than on raw AI mechanics.
- **Beyond Prompt Tricks:** Prompt hacks decay as models evolve; true fluency builds durable competencies that stay relevant across model generations.
- **Course Leadership:** Developed with Anthropic educators and staff (Joe Feller, Drew Bent, Rick Dacon, and Maggie Vo).

---

## ⚙️ The Core Framework: The "Four Ds"

1. **Delegation:** Deciding what work humans handle vs. what should be assigned to AI.
2. **Description:** Communicating vision, context, and constraints clearly.
3. **Discernment:** Evaluating, testing, and critically assessing AI outputs.
4. **Diligence:** Ensuring interactions are safe, ethical, transparent, and accountable.

## 🛑 STOP — Self-Check

**Question:** Why does Anthropic emphasize "thinking with AI" over memorizing prompt engineering tips?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

Prompt tips are tactical and temporary—they often break when models update. "Thinking with AI" is a durable, human-centric framework (governed by the 4Ds) that remains effective regardless of technological shifts.
