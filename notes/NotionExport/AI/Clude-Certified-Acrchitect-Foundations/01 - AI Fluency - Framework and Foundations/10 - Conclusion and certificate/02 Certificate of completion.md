## 🗺️ Stage 0 — Concept Map

**The problem:** Candidates often review concepts passively without testing their recall under exam conditions, leaving subtle misinterpretations of core definitions (such as confusing Process Discernment with Product Discernment, or Problem Awareness with Platform Awareness) uncorrected before the official certification exam.

**Where it fits:**
- **Validates:** Complete mastery of Course 01 (*AI Fluency: Framework and Foundations*).
- **Prerequisite for:** Passing the **Foundations Competency** section of the official *Claude Certified Architect – Foundations* exam.

**Why care as an Architect?** The certification assessment tests your ability to make rigorous distinctions between the 4D pillars. Knowing the precise official definitions ensures consistent communication during architectural reviews and client governance discussions.

---

## 🔑 Key Assessment Concepts

| Competency | Core Definition Tested | Typical Distractor Traps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AI Fluency** | Working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely | "Memorizing prompt tricks" or "Automating all tasks" |
| **The 4 Ds** | Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence | Confusing with software lifecycle (Design, Development, Deployment) |
| **Delegation** | Strategic division of labor between human and AI | Conflating with prompt drafting or quality evaluation |
| **Description** | Clear communication: Product (what), Process (how), Performance (role) | Reducing to narrow "prompt hacks" or "vending machine" commands |
| **Discernment** | Critical quality control evaluating outputs, reasoning, and behavior | Conflating with spellchecking or passive acceptance |
| **Diligence** | Ethical, safe, and transparent collaboration with human liability | Assuming the AI holds legal or ethical liability |

## 📝 Final Assessment: Question Analysis & Architectural Rationale

### Question 1 of 13
**According to the AI Fluency Framework, what does AI Fluency mean?**

- [ ] A) Memorizing the best prompts for different AI tools
- [ ] B) Using AI to automate all possible tasks
- [ ] C) Becoming a technical expert in AI development
- [x] **D) The ability to work with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically, and safely**

> **✅ Correct Answer: D**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** AI Fluency is explicitly defined by Anthropic across four balanced criteria: *Effectiveness* (quality outcomes), *Efficiency* (frictionless workflows), *Ethics* (fairness and honesty), and *Safety* (data privacy and harm prevention). Options A, B, and C represent common misconceptions—fluency is not about prompt memorization, total blind automation, or deep low-level model training.

---

### Question 2 of 13
**The four core competencies in the AI Fluency Framework are known as "The 4 Ds." Which of the following lists all four correctly?**

- [ ] A) Design, Description, Development, Deployment
- [ ] B) Data, Delegation, Discernment, Documentation
- [ ] C) Direction, Description, Decision-making, Diligence
- [x] **D) Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence**

> **✅ Correct Answer: D**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** The official Anthropic AI Fluency Framework is anchored on the 4 Ds:
> 1. **Delegation:** Deciding who does what (Strategic Planning).
> 2. **Description:** Communicating intent clearly (Inbound Specification).
> 3. **Discernment:** Evaluating quality and reasoning (Outbound Quality Control).
> 4. **Diligence:** Ensuring ethics, safety, and accountability (Systemic Governance).
> Options A, B, and C borrow conventional software development acronyms that do not represent Anthropic's human-AI collaboration framework.

### Question 3 of 13
**Which competency focuses primarily on deciding what work should be done by AI versus what should be handled by humans?**

- [ ] A) Diligence
- [x] **B) Delegation**
- [ ] C) Discernment
- [ ] D) Description

> **✅ Correct Answer: B**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** **Delegation** is the architectural division of labor. It establishes the boundary between human judgment and machine execution by evaluating tasks across *Problem Awareness*, *Platform Awareness*, and *Task Allocation* (Automation, Augmentation, Agency, or Human-Reserved).

---

### Question 4 of 13
**According to the framework, what is the primary focus of the Diligence competency?**

- [ ] A) Writing better prompts for AI systems
- [ ] B) Making AI work faster and more efficiently
- [x] **C) Responsible AI use, transparency, and accountability**
- [ ] D) Evaluating the quality of AI outputs

> **✅ Correct Answer: C**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** While Delegation and Description maximize *Effectiveness & Efficiency*, **Diligence** is the ethical, legal, and safety anchor of the framework. It governs tool selection (Creation), honest disclosure (Transparency), and personal liability for final deliverables (Deployment). Option D describes Discernment; Option A describes Description; Option B describes general efficiency.

---

### Question 5 of 13
**Which of the following best describes "Problem Awareness" within the Delegation competency?**

- [ ] A) Understanding which AI platform to use
- [ ] B) Knowing how to write effective prompts
- [ ] C) Evaluating the quality of AI outputs
- [x] **D) Clearly defining your goals and understanding what work is needed before involving AI**

> **✅ Correct Answer: D**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** Under Anthropic's "Expertise-First" principle, good delegation begins before opening an AI tool. **Problem Awareness** requires the practitioner to understand the project vision, define success criteria, and categorize the required cognitive modes (mechanical vs. ambiguous vs. critical judgment). Option A defines *Platform Awareness*; Option B defines *Description*; Option C defines *Discernment*.

---

### Question 6 of 13
**Which competency focuses on communicating clearly with AI systems about what you want, how you want it done, and how you want to interact?**

- [ ] A) Discernment
- [ ] B) Delegation
- [ ] C) Diligence
- [x] **D) Description**

> **✅ Correct Answer: D**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** **Description** is the inbound communication competency. It bridges human intentionality and machine execution across three explicit pillars: **Product** (*what* you want), **Process** (*how* to approach it), and **Performance** (*how* the model should interact and behave).

---

### Question 7 of 13
**Which of the following best describes "Product Description" within the Description competency?**

- [ ] A) Explaining which AI platform to use for a task
- [ ] B) Evaluating whether AI outputs meet your requirements
- [x] **C) Clearly defining what you want the AI to create or provide**
- [ ] D) Taking responsibility for AI-generated content accuracy

> **✅ Correct Answer: C**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** **Product Description** specifies the tangible output: the business context, the concrete task, the audience, and the target schema/format (e.g., Markdown table, OpenAPI YAML). Option B describes *Product Discernment*; Option D describes *Deployment Diligence*; Option A describes *Platform Awareness*.

---

### Question 8 of 13
**Which competency involves critically evaluating AI outputs, processes, and behaviors to ensure quality and appropriateness?**

- [ ] A) Description
- [ ] B) Delegation
- [x] **C) Discernment**
- [ ] D) Diligence

> **✅ Correct Answer: C**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** **Discernment** is your personal quality control system and the outbound flip side of Description. It audits deliverables for factual truth (**Product Discernment**), inspects the validity of the reasoning path (**Process Discernment**), and monitors collaborative friction (**Performance Discernment**).

---

### Question 9 of 13
**Which of the following best describes "Process Discernment" within the Discernment competency?**

- [ ] A) Judging the final quality of AI-generated content
- [ ] B) Deciding which AI system to use for a task
- [x] **C) Assessing whether the AI's reasoning process and problem solving approach is effective**
- [ ] D) Ensuring transparency about AI involvement in your work

> **✅ Correct Answer: C**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** **Process Discernment** focuses on *how the model got to its answer*. It audits step-by-step logic, verifies intermediate scratchpad reasoning, and detects red flags such as dropped constraints, circular proofs, and reinserted bad ideas. Option A describes *Product Discernment*; Option B describes *Platform Awareness*; Option D describes *Transparency Diligence*.

---

### Question 10 of 13
**Which of the following best describes "Transparency Diligence" within the Diligence competency?**

- [ ] A) Ensuring AI outputs are technically accurate
- [x] **B) Being open and honest about AI's role in your work with those who need to know**
- [ ] C) Making AI work as fast as possible
- [ ] D) Choosing the most advanced AI system available

> **✅ Correct Answer: B**  
> **Architectural Rationale:** **Transparency Diligence** is about building and preserving stakeholder trust by forthrightly communicating how AI was used, what tasks it assisted with, and meeting disclosure expectations across academic, professional, and enterprise settings. Option A describes *Product Discernment / Deployment Diligence*; Option D relates to *Creation Diligence*.
